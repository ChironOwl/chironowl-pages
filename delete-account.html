Delete your Chiron Owl account

Chiron Owl accounts are optional and only keep your chapter ratings and purchases with you.

In the app

  1. Open Chiron Owl and tap the settings icon on the Home screen.
  2. Tap Delete my account, then confirm with Delete.

Without the app

Email andreaposadino@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Chiron Owl account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account (name, email and profile picture link received from Google) your chapter ratings, our record of your purchases and any old leaderboard entry are deleted permanently. Purchases themselves stay with your Google account and can be restored. Anonymous usage statistics are never linked to your account, so there is nothing else to delete. Progress saved on your phone is not affected; uninstalling the app removes it.
