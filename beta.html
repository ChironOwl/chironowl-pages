Chiron Owl — beta testers wanted

Chiron Owl is an Android learning game about philosophy, history and art, with over 1,000 cards: from the Presocratics and the Roman Empire to Renaissance painting, Ancient Egypt and Impressionism. Each card asks a question and you answer with a swipe: left or right to answer, down to skip. Cards you miss come back later (spaced repetition), cards you get right go into your collection, and a timeline links events across topics.

I'm a solo developer. Before Google Play lets the app into the store, it has to run a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days, and I'd like honest feedback from people who enjoy philosophy, history or art.

What testing asks of you

  • An Android phone and a Google account.
  • Stay opted in for 14 days. Leaving early restarts the count for Google, so please keep the app installed even if you only open it now and then.
  • Play a few chapters and try what you like: the swipe cards, the other game modes, the collection, the timeline, rating a chapter.
  • At the end, answer a short survey (a few questions, about 3 minutes).

During the test every topic is unlocked at no cost, and nothing is ever charged unless you choose to buy. After launch the app will be freemium: some topics stay free, and the others come as topic packs or a subscription. There are no rewards for testing; you just get to shape the app before it launches.

How to join (2 steps)

1. Join the testers group with the Google account you use on your phone. You won't get spam: the group exists only so Google Play can recognise testers.
