Chiron Owl — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Chiron Owl is a learning game about philosophy, history and art. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: you can play without an account, the app collects no advertising data, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Data stored only on your device

Your progress (answers, streaks, collected cards) is saved on your phone. It is not sent to us.

Optional account (Sign in with Google)

Signing in is optional. It keeps your chapter ratings and your purchases with your account. If you sign in with Google, we receive from Google your name, email address and profile picture link, and we create an account identifier. Nothing about you is shown to other players. Earlier versions of the app stored a points total on a public leaderboard; the app no longer has one, and those totals will be deleted.

Purchases (Premium and topic packs)

Purchases are made through Google Play, which handles payment; we never see your card or payment details. To unlock what you bought on any device, we keep a record of each purchase with your account: the product (Premium or a topic pack), its status (for example active, cancelled or refunded), when a subscription ends or renews, and the purchase token Google Play gives us to check it. Buying therefore needs an account. You can cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play.

Daily chapter reminder

If you turn it on, the app shows one notification a day with the title of today's chapter. It is scheduled on your phone; no notification service or push token is involved, and you can turn it off or change its time in Settings.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which chapters and game modes work, the app sends anonymous events such as "chapter started", "question answered", "intro page skipped", "link followed", "daily reminder opened", "read aloud played", "field page opened", "store opened" or "purchase completed" (the product only, never a price or payment detail), with the content id involved, the app version and the date. Each installation has a random identifier that is not linked to your account, name or email. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage stats; turning it off also deletes events not yet sent.

Where data is stored

Account, chapter ratings, purchase records and usage data are stored with our backend provider, Supabase. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We do not sell data or share it with third parties for advertising. Google processes sign-in under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, any old leaderboard entry, your chapter ratings and our record of your purchases (the purchases themselves stay with your Google account, so you can restore them later with a new account). Progress saved on your device stays until you uninstall the app. You can also ask us to delete your account by email — see how to delete your account.

Children

Chiron Owl is not directed at children under 13.
